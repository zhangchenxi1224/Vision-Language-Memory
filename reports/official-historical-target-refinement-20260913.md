# Fixed historical target refinement

The actual hash-selected16 targets contain two known strict-readback failures: juice retrieval on one rewrite, and `no preference` instead of `no active preference` on another target. Their full prior evidence remains intact. This experiment refines the existing deterministic selection for every question; it does not substitute targets after inspecting Reader outcomes.

`refine_historical_writer_targets.py` binds the complete64-target bank SHA256 `d54895adb15b91c3befd52c57f6248c76cf2abadb44916af830726a0736e688c` and writes its exact16-target plan before starting either lane. Each target starts from its selected historical step256 latent, with fresh Adam, seed0 and256 additional updates. The previously validated recipe uses learning rate0.05, betas0.9/0.999, epsilon1e-8, no weight decay, and mean answer CE plus assistant-EOS CE. Original-open, paraphrase1 and paraphrase2 rotate from update zero. The parent and cumulative512 latent updates are explicit; a fresh optimizer is not a fresh latent initialization.

Only one FP32 latent per independent target is trainable. The FP32 Base VAE and bf16 Qwen3-VL Reader are frozen and bound to the already verified snapshots, including exact Base/Mobile VAE equivalence. No U-Net or Writer is loaded or optimized. Actual repeated gradient equality is required before each target's first update. Every latent step is saved, together with the existing fixed optimizer/RNG checkpoints. A finite deadline is checked during optimization and readback. Partial evidence remains on failure; there is no automatic restart or endpoint selection.

Two independent lanes use target index modulo2 and explicitly assigned idle H200s. They do not synchronize gradients and do not change the already registered Writer validation. The intended free devices are2 and3; creation fails if either is occupied. The user's instance is kept running.

Each fixed endpoint is evaluated with allfive original query strings in both FP32-decoded and rounded RGB forms. Greedy raw32-token outputs must equal the exact gold tokens immediately followed by EOS151645. Blank and the unchanged metadata-selected donor are evaluated in both forms but are never counted as matched successes. All480 raw records,160 matched records, images, trajectories, initial hashes and artifacts are retained. The original64 teachers remain in the parent bank. A new16-target bank is sealed only if every target passes allten matched reads; failed targets are not discarded and their raw outputs are retained even when no bank is sealed.

Allfive queries have already been observed in historical diagnostic readback. Two queries are excluded from this optimization, but none is an untouched research holdout. Successful teacher qualification would only prepare broader shared-Writer training. The resulting full-prefix gray-source task still needs actual shared-Writer training/inference and separate continuous-memory evidence.
